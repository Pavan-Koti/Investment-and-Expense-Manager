Google Drive Setup Guide

To grant this app permission to create its sync file in your Google Drive:

  1. Go to Google Cloud Console and enable Google Drive API.
  2. Under OAuth consent screen > Scopes, add https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata.
  3. Add your email under Test users.
  4. Under Credentials, add your local origin URL (e.g. http://localhost:8000).
